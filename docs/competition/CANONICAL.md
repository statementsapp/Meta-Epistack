# Canonical Document Policy

The files under [`docs/competition/`](.) are the **canonical** constraints for
work in this repository. They define what FLF’s Epistemic Stack vision and
Epistemic Case Study Competition ask for, how entries are judged, and how
submissions must be shaped.

## Canonical set

| Document | Role |
|---|---|
| [epistack.md](epistack.md) | Vision and objectives of the epistemic stack |
| [epistack-competition.md](epistack-competition.md) | Competition brief, desiderata, case studies, prizes |
| [judging-criteria.md](judging-criteria.md) | Scoring dimensions and prize tiers ([PDF](judging-criteria.pdf)) |
| [format-and-length.md](format-and-length.md) | Judge attention budget and submission format ([PDF](format-and-length.pdf)) |

Prefer the live FLF pages for wording updates:

- https://flf.org/epistack/
- https://flf.org/epistack-competition/

If a local mirror and the live page diverge, treat the **live page** (or the
PDF appendix, for judging/format) as authoritative and update the mirror.

## Policy

1. **Constraint, not inspiration.** Designs, workflows, prototypes, protocols,
   and writeups in this repo should be compatible with the canonical set unless
   an explicit, documented exception is made.
2. **Warn on conflict.** When a proposed idea, plan, or change appears to
   contravene the vision, competition brief, judging criteria, or format rules,
   surface the conflict **before** implementing or expanding on it. Name the
   conflicting document and the specific tension.
3. **Do not silently override.** Do not “improve” away competition constraints
   (e.g. skipping case demonstrations, collapsing assessment into opaque
   narrative, ignoring compounding/shareability, or blowing past the reading
   budget without a navigable entry point) without flagging it.
4. **Exceptions are deliberate.** If we intentionally diverge (e.g. exploratory
   R&D beyond the prize bar), say so in writing and keep the divergence scoped.

## What “contravene” usually looks like

Examples that should trigger a warning (not exhaustive):

- Optimizing only for a single-user artifact that does not travel, combine, or
  survive scrutiny (vs compounding & shareability)
- Treating assessment as the shared/objective layer, or flattening provenance
  and structure away (vs late-binding assessment; objective structural layers)
- Overfitting to one case study with no plan to generalize
- A polished demo with opaque methodology (vs methodological transparency)
- Core written engagement that ignores the ~10-page judge attention budget
  without designating what judges should read first
- Claiming to “decide the truth” rather than supporting investigation of
  arguments, evidence, and provenance

Agents and collaborators should check proposals against this policy and the
canonical set, and warn when there is a material conflict.
